Privacy Policy

Your trust, our commitment
Effective Date: August 2026  ·  Last Updated: August 2026

Welcome to SissyTrends Boutique. We are a boutique based in Coimbatore, Tamil Nadu, India, curating handpicked sarees, imitation jewellery, and festive décor. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal information when you interact with us — through our website, WhatsApp, social media, or any online advertising forms.

By submitting your information through any of our forms or contacting us, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

1. Information We Collect

When you interact with SissyTrends — including through Meta (Facebook/Instagram) lead forms or our website — we may collect:

  • Contact details: Name, phone number, email address
  • Delivery information: Postal address, city, state, pincode
  • Order information: Products you enquire about or purchase
  • Communication data: Messages you send via WhatsApp or our contact form
  • Location data: Approximate location, only if you grant permission
  • Device & usage data: Browser type, pages visited, time spent (collected automatically via standard web analytics)

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information solely to serve you better:

  • To process and fulfil your orders
  • To contact you about your enquiries or orders via WhatsApp or phone
  • To send you order confirmations and shipping updates
  • To respond to your questions and provide customer support
  • To show you relevant offers and products (only if you have opted in)
  • To improve our website and shopping experience

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to any third party for their own marketing purposes.

3. Meta Lead Forms (Facebook & Instagram Ads)

When you submit information through a Meta (Facebook or Instagram) lead advertisement form, your data is collected by Meta on our behalf and shared with us. By submitting the form, you consent to:

  • SissyTrends receiving and using your contact details to reach out to you about the product or offer you expressed interest in
  • Being contacted via WhatsApp, phone call, or SMS in connection with your enquiry

Meta's own data practices are governed by the Meta Privacy Policy.

4. Payment Information

All online payments are processed securely through Razorpay, a PCI-DSS compliant payment gateway. We do not store your card details, UPI credentials, or banking information on our servers at any point.

5. Data Sharing

We share your information only in the following limited circumstances:

  • Delivery partners: Your name and address are shared with courier/shipping services to fulfil your order
  • Payment processors: Razorpay receives transaction details to process payments
  • Legal requirements: If required by law, court order, or government authority

We do not share your data with advertisers or data brokers.

6. Data Retention

We retain your personal information for as long as necessary to fulfil your order, provide customer support, and comply with our legal obligations. If you request deletion of your data, we will remove it within 30 days, except where retention is required by law.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Opt out of marketing communications at any time
  • Withdraw consent for us to contact you

To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details below.

8. Cookies & Analytics

Our website may use cookies and similar technologies to understand how visitors use the site and to improve your experience. You can control cookie settings through your browser. We do not use cookies to track you across other websites for advertising purposes.

9. Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your personal information against unauthorised access, loss, or misuse. However, no internet transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10. Children's Privacy

Our services are not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has submitted information to us, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
